About ReclaimWealth

Last updated: July 25, 2026

ReclaimWealth helps people find retirement money they left behind at old jobs — and then get it back into their own hands. We are a private company, not a government agency, and everything we do is built around one uncomfortable fact: most of what we help with, you could do yourself for free. We think you should know that before you decide whether we are worth paying.

Why this exists

When people change jobs, retirement accounts get left behind. Not through carelessness — a 401(k) stays with an old employer's plan, the company gets acquired, the recordkeeper changes, statements stop arriving at an address you moved out of a decade ago. Years later there is real money sitting somewhere with your name on it, and no obvious thread to pull.

Research by Capitalize with the Center for Retirement Research at Boston College put the scale of it at roughly $2.1 trillion across about 31.9 million forgotten 401(k) accounts, with an average balance near $66,691 — about a quarter of all 401(k) assets in the country.

The information needed to find these accounts is public. It is just scattered across federal registries, fifty separate state programs, and a filing database most people have never heard of, each with different rules about what it covers and what it demands from you. That gap between "public" and "findable" is the entire reason this service exists.

How we make money

Searching is free. We charge only if you want us to do the legwork.

  • Flat, disclosed fees. Our paid plans have fixed prices published on the site before you buy anything.
  • Never a percentage of your money. We do not take contingency fees. Our fee is identical whether you recover nothing or two hundred thousand dollars.
  • We never sell your personal information. Not to advertisers, not to data brokers, not to anyone.
  • Referral fees get disclosed. If we ever earn a fee for introducing you to a third party, we tell you at the moment of the referral — before you act on it.

We would rather you found your money for free using our free links and state guides and told someone about us, than paid us for something you did not need.

What we will not do

  • We will not tell you that we found money before it has actually been verified.
  • We will not manufacture urgency. In most states unclaimed property is held indefinitely — nobody is about to take it from you.
  • We will not ask for your Social Security number or bank logins by email or text.
  • We will not take custody of your funds or file claims in your name.
  • We will not give financial, tax, or legal advice. When you need that, you need a licensed professional, and we will say so.
