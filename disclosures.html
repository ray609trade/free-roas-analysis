Disclosures

Last updated: July 25, 2026

This page states plainly what ReclaimWealth is, what it is not, and how we make money. We put it in one place because this is an industry where people get taken advantage of, and you deserve to know exactly who you are dealing with before you give anyone your information.

We are not a government agency

ReclaimWealth is a private, independent company. We are not affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by, or acting on behalf of:

  • The U.S. Department of Labor or the Employee Benefits Security Administration
  • The Pension Benefit Guaranty Corporation (PBGC)
  • The Internal Revenue Service or the Social Security Administration
  • Any state treasury, comptroller, or unclaimed property office
  • Any retirement plan, plan administrator, recordkeeper, employer, or financial institution

Any company or institution named on this site is named only to help you identify where your account may be held. Those names are the property of their owners, and their use here does not imply any relationship with us.

You can do all of this yourself, for free

You never need to pay anyone — including us — to claim money that belongs to you.

Every registry we use is free and open to the public:

  • DOL Retirement Savings Lost & Found — free
  • National Registry of Unclaimed Retirement Benefits — free
  • PBGC Unclaimed Pensions — free
  • State unclaimed property — free
  • DOL Form 5500 filings — free
  • DOL Benefits Advisors — free one-on-one federal help

No state unclaimed property office and no retirement plan charges you to file a claim for your own property. If you would rather do the work yourself, use the links above and our free state guides — you will not hurt our feelings, and you will keep every dollar.

How we make money

Straightforwardly, and only in ways we will always disclose:

  • Optional paid plans. Fixed, disclosed-in-advance fees for us to do the legwork — running the multi-state sweep, tracing plan administrators through Form 5500 filings, assembling your claim packet, and monitoring for new matches. Prices are on our pricing section.
  • We do not charge contingency or percentage fees. We never take a cut of what you recover. Our fee is the same whether you find $0 or $200,000.
  • We do not sell your personal information — not to advertisers, not to data brokers, not to anyone. See our Privacy Policy.
  • If we ever earn a referral fee from a third party — for example if we introduce you to a provider who can help you roll over an account you have recovered — we will disclose that clearly at the point of referral, before you act on it.

No guarantee, and no way to know in advance

We cannot tell you whether you have unclaimed retirement money before the official registries are searched, and neither can anybody else. Private retirement balances are protected by law and are not searchable by name. Any service that claims to have already found money for you, before you have verified your identity with the holding institution, is not being straight with you.

The statistics on this site — including that roughly $2.1 trillion sits in about 31.9 million forgotten 401(k) accounts — describe the U.S. population in aggregate. They come from 2025 research by Capitalize with the Center for Retirement Research at Boston College. They say nothing about your personal situation.

State regulation of paid recovery assistance

Many states regulate people who, for a fee, help others locate or recover unclaimed property. Those rules can include registration requirements, caps on fees, mandatory contract terms, and limits on when an agreement may be made after property is reported to the state.

We take these seriously. We offer paid services only where we are permitted to, our fees are flat rather than a share of your recovery, and we disclose on every page that you may claim your property yourself at no cost. We may decline or limit paid services for residents of particular states, and where we cannot charge lawfully, we will not charge. Free use of our information and guides remains available everywhere.

Advertising and claims

We commit to advertising this service honestly: no manufactured urgency about your own money, no implication that funds have been found before they have, no fake testimonials, and no claim we cannot substantiate. Every statistic we publish carries a source and a date. If you ever see us fall short of that, tell us at info@rmgcredit.com and we will correct it.

Watch out for scams

Because this field attracts bad actors, a few warning signs worth knowing — whoever you use:

  • Anyone asking for your Social Security number or bank login by email or text
  • Anyone claiming to be from a government agency and demanding a fee
  • Anyone saying your money will be lost if you do not act immediately — in most states unclaimed property is held indefinitely
  • Anyone asking for payment by gift card, wire transfer, or cryptocurrency
  • Anyone who will not tell you their fee in writing before you agree
