How to find an old TIAA 401(k)

Dominant in higher education, hospitals, and non-profits. If you worked for a university or a non-profit, start here.

TIAA does not charge you to recover your own account. Contacting them directly is free. ReclaimWealth is not affiliated with TIAA; the details below are published publicly by the company.

Step by step

  1. Try the participant portal first. Use the account lookup link above. Many former employees can re-register with their Social Security number and date of birth even if they never had a login.
  2. Call if the portal doesn't recognise you. Have ready: your Social Security number, dates of employment, and the exact legal name of your former employer.
  3. If they have no record, confirm who holds the plan now. Recordkeepers change. Search the DOL Form 5500 filings for your former employer to see who administers the plan today.
  4. Check whether the balance was cashed out. If the account was closed and escheated, the money is with a state — see our state guides.
  5. Escalate if you are stonewalled. DOL Benefits Advisors help for free.
