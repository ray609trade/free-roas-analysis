Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 24, 2026

ReclaimWealth ("we," "us," or "our") operates a search-assistance service that helps individuals locate potential lost or unclaimed retirement accounts. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using our website and submitting your information, you agree to the practices described here.

The short version: We collect only what we need to help you search for lost retirement accounts, we use it only to perform and follow up on that search, and we never sell your personal information.

1. Information We Collect

When you use our search form, we collect the information you choose to provide, which may include:

  • Your first and last name
  • Your email address and, optionally, your phone number
  • The states you have lived or worked in
  • The names of your former employers or companies

We do not ask for your Social Security number, account numbers, or financial-institution login credentials in order to begin a search. Please do not send us that information by email.

We may also automatically collect limited technical information (such as browser type, device, and general location) through standard web logs and, if enabled, analytics tools.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To perform or assist with a search of public and national registries for potential retirement accounts associated with you;
  • To contact you about the results of that search and to help you claim any funds that may belong to you;
  • To respond to your questions and provide customer support;
  • To operate, maintain, secure, and improve our website; and
  • To comply with applicable laws and enforce our Terms of Service.

3. How We Share Information

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We may share it only in these limited circumstances:

  • Service providers who help us operate the site and communicate with you (for example, form-processing or email providers), bound to protect your information;
  • With your direction or consent, such as when we help you contact a plan administrator or institution on your behalf;
  • Legal and safety reasons, when required by law or to protect our rights, users, or the public.

4. Third-Party Registries and Links

Our service directs you to independent government and national registries (such as the U.S. Department of Labor, PBGC, and state unclaimed-property programs). We do not control those websites, and their use of your information is governed by their own privacy policies. We encourage you to review them.

5. Data Retention

We keep your information only as long as needed to provide our service, follow up with you, and meet legal and record-keeping obligations, after which we delete or de-identify it.

6. Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect the information you provide. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your Choices and Rights

  • You may ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal information you have provided;
  • You may opt out of marketing communications at any time by following the unsubscribe instructions or contacting us;
  • Depending on where you live (for example, California or the EU/UK), you may have additional rights over your personal data. Contact us to exercise them.

8. Children's Privacy

Our service is intended for adults and is not directed to children under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes take effect when posted, and the "Last updated" date above will reflect the revision.

10. Contact Us

Questions about this policy or your information? Email us at info@rmgcredit.com.
