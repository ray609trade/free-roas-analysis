Find your old 401(k) by recordkeeper

Most lost 401(k) accounts are sitting with one of a handful of large recordkeeping firms. If you can remember — or work out — who administered your old plan, you can often get straight back into the account with a phone call.

Contacting a recordkeeper is always free. ReclaimWealth is not affiliated with any company listed here; these are public contact details.
