Pricing questions

Do you take a percentage of what I recover?

No. We charge flat fees, disclosed before you pay, and we never take a cut of recovered funds. We also never take custody of your money — it goes directly from the state or the plan administrator to you. Anyone offering to find your money for "just a small percentage" is operating under finder statutes that cap those fees, and in several states that arrangement is restricted or prohibited outright.

Does the $19 plan turn into a subscription?

No. It is a single charge that does not renew. Only the $29 monthly plan renews, and you can cancel it online in one click at any time — no phone call, no retention script.

What if you don't find anything?

That happens, and we tell you plainly when it does. We can't guarantee unclaimed funds exist for any individual — nobody honestly can. What you're paying for is the search being run properly across all fifty states and every employer you list, and a written record of what was checked. If we fail to deliver that report, email us and we'll refund you in full.

Can I just do all of this myself for free?

Yes, and we'd genuinely rather you did if you have the time. Every registry we use is free and public — they're all linked on our homepage, and our state guides walk through each one. The paid plans exist for people who don't want to spend an evening on it, not because there's a paywall in front of your own money.

How do I cancel?

One click from the link in any receipt email, or email info@rmgcredit.com and we'll do it the same business day. See our Terms of Service for the full cancellation and refund terms.
