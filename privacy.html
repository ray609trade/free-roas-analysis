1. Who we are

ReclaimWealth ("we," "us," or "our") operates a search-assistance service that helps people locate potentially lost or unclaimed retirement accounts. This policy explains how we handle personal information collected through our website and services. It applies to our website and to communications we send you.

3. What we never ask for

To start a search we do not ask for, and you should not send us, your Social Security number, your date of birth, financial account numbers, or usernames and passwords for any financial institution or government site. Official registries such as the Department of Labor's Retirement Savings Lost & Found require you to verify your identity directly with them — never through us. Be cautious of any service that asks you for those details by email.

4. How we use your information

  • To search publicly available registries and records relevant to your work history;
  • To prepare and send your search plan and results;
  • To contact you about your search, your account, and — only with your separate consent — by phone or text;
  • To provide, bill for, and support any paid plan you purchase;
  • To maintain records of consent and transactions as required by law;
  • To operate, secure, debug, and improve the Service and prevent fraud and abuse;
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms of Service.

5. How we share information

We share personal information only in these limited circumstances:

  • Service providers who operate the Service on our behalf — for example form processing, email delivery, payment processing, hosting, and analytics — who are permitted to use it only to perform services for us;
  • At your direction, such as when you ask us to help you contact a plan administrator or state office;
  • Legal and safety reasons: to comply with law or valid legal process, to enforce our Terms, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of our users, the public, or us;
  • Business transfer: if we are involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, information may transfer as part of that transaction, subject to this policy.

6. We do not sell your information

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, as those terms are defined under California and other state privacy laws. We have not done so in the preceding twelve months. We do not sell or share the personal information of any person we know to be under 16.

8. Phone, text and consent records

Providing a phone number is optional. We will only call or text you for marketing purposes if you gave express written consent by ticking the separate, optional consent box — and that consent is never a condition of purchasing or using any service.

When you give that consent we record the date and time, the page, your browser user agent, the consent version, and the exact wording you were shown. We keep those records to prove consent was properly obtained, and we keep them after you withdraw consent for the same reason.

To stop calls or texts: reply STOP to any text, tell us during a call, or email info@rmgcredit.com. We honor requests promptly and maintain an internal do-not-call list. Reply HELP to any text for help. Message frequency varies; message and data rates may apply. Mobile opt-in data is not shared with third parties for their own marketing.

9. Email and marketing choices

You can unsubscribe from marketing email at any time using the link in any message, or by emailing us. We honor unsubscribe requests within 10 business days, and our commercial emails include our physical mailing address as the law requires. We will still send transactional messages relating to a purchase or an active request.

10. Cookies and analytics

We use a small number of cookies and similar technologies to make the site work and to understand how it is used in aggregate. We do not use them to build advertising profiles about you. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings; essential functionality may be affected. We honor Global Privacy Control browser signals where applicable law requires it.

11. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Know / access the categories and specific pieces of personal information we have collected about you, the sources, and the purposes;
  • Correct inaccurate personal information;
  • Delete personal information we hold about you, subject to legal exceptions such as records we must keep to evidence consent or for tax purposes;
  • Opt out of sale or sharing for targeted advertising — which we do not do in any event;
  • Limit use of sensitive personal information — we do not collect sensitive personal information to begin a search;
  • Non-discrimination for exercising any of these rights. We will not deny you service, charge a different price, or give you a lesser experience because you exercised a privacy right.

How to exercise them: email info@rmgcredit.com with the words "Privacy Request" in the subject line. We will verify your identity — usually by confirming information you already gave us — and respond within the timeframe required by applicable law, generally 45 days, with one permitted extension where necessary. An authorized agent may submit a request on your behalf with proof of authorization. If we decline a request, we will tell you why, and you may appeal by replying to our response.

12. Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards appropriate to the sensitivity of the information we hold, including transport encryption (HTTPS) and limiting access to those who need it. We use a third-party payment processor and do not receive or store full payment card numbers. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

13. Third-party registries

Our service directs you to independent government and third-party registries, including the U.S. Department of Labor, the PBGC, and state unclaimed property programs. We do not control those sites, and any information you provide to them is governed by their privacy policies, which we encourage you to read — particularly where they require identity verification.

14. Children's privacy

The Service is intended for adults and is not directed to children under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

15. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will revise the "Last updated" date above, and for material changes we will provide more prominent notice. Continued use after the effective date means you accept the updated policy.
